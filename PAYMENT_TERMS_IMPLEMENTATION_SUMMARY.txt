HOW TO USE:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1: Open PO creation page
Step 2: Scroll to Financial Details section
Step 3: Click ✅ Add Payment Terms & Conditions (optional)
Step 4: Select any/all 4 payment term checkboxes
Step 5: Optionally add custom text in "Custom Payment Terms" field
Step 6: Submit form - terms automatically saved

When Editing:
- If payment terms exist, checkboxes auto-populate
- Section auto-expands
- You can modify before saving
