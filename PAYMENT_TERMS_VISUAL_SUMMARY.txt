HOW IT WORKS:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CREATE MODE:
  1. Click ✅ Add Payment Terms & Conditions checkbox
  2. Section expands showing 4 payment term options
  3. Check any/all that apply:
     ☑ 1️⃣ Not returnable
     ☑ 2️⃣ Payment schedule
     ☐ 3️⃣ No liability delays
     ☑ 4️⃣ Invoice verified
  4. Optionally add custom text: "FOB Shipping, Net 30"
  5. Submit form
  6. Saved as: "notReturnable,paymentSchedule,invoiceVerified"

EDIT MODE:
  1. Open existing PO for editing
  2. Payment Terms section automatically expands
  3. Checkboxes auto-populate based on saved terms
  4. Modify if needed
  5. Save changes
  6. Data persists
